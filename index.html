How a week works

Order Thursday. Eat all week.

01 / 04

  1. 01 · Early in the week

    The new menu goes live

    New meals every week, posted on the website. Fall brings the soups back.

  2. 02 · By Thursday, 6 PM

    Pick your meals

    Add what you want to your cart and check out online. Ordering closes Thursday at 6 PM for the following Tuesday.

  3. 03 · Monday & Tuesday

    We cook and seal

    Everything is made from fresh ingredients in a certified kitchen and sealed for freshness.

  4. 04 · Tuesday

    Your door, or the cooler

    Delivered to your door, or grab yours from the cooler at Ironsmith's Gym. Then heat for 2–3 minutes.

The chef

From El Salvador, by way of fine-dining kitchens, to Crawfordsville.

Sergio Landaverde grew up around the pupusas, tamales and stews of his family’s kitchen. He spent years as a career chef in high-end restaurant kitchens before starting Casa Fresca to cook for his own community.
