FAQ

Good questions

Still wondering? Call or text (317) 294-2433.

When do I need to order?

By Thursday at 6 PM for delivery or pickup the following Tuesday. The countdown at the top of this site shows how long is left.

When is the new menu posted?

Early in the week before. Casa Fresca also posts on Facebook when the menu is live and when new dishes are added.

How do I heat the meals?

Two to three minutes in the microwave.

Are the meals frozen?

No. Meals are made with fresh, never-frozen ingredients and sealed for freshness.

Can I choose my base?

On many bowls, yes: white rice, brown rice (about 50¢ more) or quinoa (about $1 more). Pasta dishes can sometimes swap to quinoa.

Where can I see nutrition information?

Many meals have a printed nutrition label. It’s on each meal here on the menu page and in the product photos on casafrescameals.com.

Can I pick up instead of delivery?

Yes. Meals are stocked in the cooler at Ironsmith's Gym, 1714 E Main St, Crawfordsville, until they sell out.

Do you ever take a week off?

Occasionally, for family time. Weeks off are announced on Facebook, and the gym cooler is usually still stocked.

How do I pay?

Online at checkout on casafrescameals.com. Major cards and Shop Pay are accepted.

Do you cater?

Yes: corporate meals, rehearsal dinners, weddings and parties. See the catering page to send the details of your event.
